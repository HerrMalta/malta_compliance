# VAT return of an Article 10 registration in Malta

Legal basis of the tax categories of the report "VAT Return Malta" for a person registered under article 10 of the Value Added Tax Act: the boxes of the return form, the purchases and supplies each sub-line holds, the rate of a reverse charge, and the cases in which self-charged VAT is not deductible. Each statement cites the provision or the official guidance it rests on. Points that no source states are listed in [Readings](#readings) with the reading applied and its grounds.

## The return form

The return of an article 10 registration is VAT Form 003/2011 (S.L. 406.09, reg. 3 and Fourth Schedule). The form has boxes 1 to 42 without sub-lines. The report adds the sub-lines of a tax accountant's worksheet, which split a box by rate or by the deduction of its VAT; each category enters a box and, where the box has sub-lines, one of them.

## Acquisitions and reverse charges (boxes 3 to 17)

- **Box 3**: intra-community acquisitions of goods, including "goods acquired by the acquirer for personal use", and services "deemed to take place where the customer is established received from taxable persons registered in other EU Member States where the customer is liable to account for the VAT in Malta" (return notes, p. 2).
- **Box 4**: services and goods "received from a supplier in another EU Member State or in any country outside the EU and which supplies are taxable in Malta including also similar supplies acquired by the business for personal use" (return notes, p. 2).
- **Boxes 6 and 7**: the tax chargeable at the Maltese rate on the value in box 3 and box 4 (return notes, p. 2).
- **Boxes 9, 9A, 10 and 11**: the acquisitions and services of boxes 3 and 4 "to the extent that the taxable person has the right of deduction and provided that such goods are used exclusively for business"; box 9 holds goods for resale and other goods, box 9A services from other Member States, box 10 capital goods, box 11 the supplies of box 4 (return notes, p. 2). Boxes 13 to 15 hold the tax on these values, adjusted where the inputs relate to exempt supplies without credit (return notes, pp. 2-3).
- **Box 17**: box 8 less box 16 (return notes, p. 3). With the full right of deduction "a NIL VAT balance will result" (return presentation). The VAT of an acquisition or reverse charge without the right of deduction is therefore in box 3/6 or 4/7 only and is payable through box 17. The leaflets state the same for acquisitions not for business use or for exempt supplies ("resulting in a balance of output VAT to be paid") and for services used partly for the business ("you have to pay VAT on the remaining part of the service").
- **Capital goods**: an intra-community acquisition of a capital good is in boxes 3 and 10. Box 30 holds "all Capital Goods purchased on which VAT was paid in Malta. Include also all importations from third countries" (return presentation); boxes 27 and 28 hold the imports of goods for resale in the same way.
- **VAT of another Member State**: purchases on which VAT is charged in the other Member State "should not be reported" (return notes, domestic purchases and imports).

## Deduction of input tax

- **Input tax** is the tax on supplies made to the person, on intra-community acquisitions and on importations "to the extent that the supplies so made and the goods so acquired or imported have been or are intended to be wholly used by him in the course or furtherance of his economic activity" (VAT Act art. 22(2)). The credit is subject to the Tenth Schedule (art. 22(5)). Self-charged VAT is deductible only when reported as due in the return (Tenth Schedule, item 2(2)(b)).
- **Blocked input tax** (Tenth Schedule, item 3(1)): no amount is input tax to the extent that it is tax on "the supply to that person or the intra-community acquisition or importation by that person of" tobacco and tobacco products, alcoholic beverages, works of art, collectors' items and antiques, motor vehicles, vessels or aircraft (with their hire and leasing), and goods and services for repairing, maintaining, fuelling and keeping them; on goods and services used for "receptions, entertainment or hospitality" except where provided for consideration in the normal course of the economic activity; and on transport or entertainment of employees, except vehicles with at least seven seats. Item 3(2) excludes goods for resale, vehicles, vessels and aircraft used for carriage for consideration or for hire, self-drive hire and driving instruction, and goods vehicles with seating normally adjacent to the driver or for nine persons or more.
- **Exempt supplies**: input tax exclusively attributable to exempt supplies without credit is not allowed (Tenth Schedule, item 5(2)).
- **Partial use**: goods or services used partly for the economic activity give input tax in the proportion of that use (Tenth Schedule, item 4(1)).

Categories: the purchases of the first two points use "VAT Box 3/6 EU purchases not deductible 18%" or "5%" and "VAT Box 4/7 Non-EU services not deductible"; goods acquired for private use use "VAT Box 3/6 EU goods for private use not deductible" or its 5% category, and services of box 4 for private use "VAT Box 4/7 Non-EU services for private use not deductible".

## Rates

- Every taxable supply is taxed at 18%, the supplies of the Eighth Schedule at its reduced rates (VAT Act art. 19(1) and (2)). An intra-community acquisition of goods is taxed at the rate of a supply of like goods in Malta (art. 19(3)). Tax is charged "on every supply of goods or of services that takes place in Malta" (art. 4(a)); for a supply by a person not established in Malta, art. 20(2) makes the payment of the tax a liability of the person to whom the supply is made and leaves the rate of art. 19.
- **5%** (Eighth Schedule): electricity (item 2), confectionery and the sugar products listed (3), medical accessories (4), printed matter, including books and electronic publications (5), items for the exclusive use of disabled persons (6), the importation of works of art, collectors' items and antiques (7), minor repairs of bicycles, shoes, leather goods and clothing (8), domestic care services (9), and admission to cultural and entertainment events (10).
- **7%**: accommodation in licensed premises (item 1) and the use of sporting facilities (11).
- **12%**: custody and management of securities (12), management of credit and credit guarantees by a person other than the grantor (13), short-term hire of pleasure boats (14), and care of the human body by regulated health professionals (15).

Categories at 5% exist for goods for resale, capital goods and services of boxes 9a and 11; categories at 12% for services of boxes 9a and 11. The goods of the Eighth Schedule acquired from another Member State at 5% are items 2 to 6; works of art acquired from another Member State are at 18%, because item 7 covers importation only.

## Exempt acquisitions

The intra-community acquisitions of Part Three of the Fifth Schedule are exempt (VAT Act art. 9(1)(b)), among them "the intra-community acquisition of goods the supply of which if made by a taxable person in Malta would in all circumstances be an exempt supply" (item 2), such as pharmaceutical goods and the medical items of Part One, and "the intra-Community acquisition of food" (item 5). Maltese law has no rate of 0%; the sub-line "RC 0%" of box 9 holds these acquisitions of goods for resale, category "VAT Box 3/6 9/13 EU goods for resale exempt 0%".

## Supplies whose VAT the customer pays (tax in danger)

The Commissioner may "direct that the payment of the tax on a taxable supply made to a person established in Malta and registered under articles 10 or 12 shall be a liability of the person to whom the supply is made" (VAT Act, Fourteenth Schedule, Part Five, item 1; art. 57). The supplier's invoice then shows, instead of the VAT amount, "Direction for customer to self-charge VAT in terms of Part Five of the Fourteenth Schedule to the VAT Act" (item 3). The supply remains taxable at 18%; category "VAT Box 18 Malta tax in danger customer liable".

## Non-deductible VAT in the books

- An expense: the template of a category without deduction books the self-charged VAT on 5960 Non-recoverable VAT.
- A fixed asset: the cost of an asset is determined in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles (S.L. 123.01, rule 6); these include "import duties and non-refundable purchase taxes" in the cost of property, plant and equipment (S.L. 281.05, Schedule, section 7.4(a); section 11.4(a) for intangible assets). The VAT is booked on the fixed asset account of the asset and is part of the cost of the Asset and of its capital allowances.

## Not covered

- Partial deduction: a purchase used partly for the economic activity (Tenth Schedule, item 4) or for both taxable and exempt supplies (items 6 and 8). A category enters the whole tax base of an invoice in its boxes.
- The customer side of a direction under Fourteenth Schedule, Part Five: no source names the box of the customer's self-charged VAT; the note on box 4 covers suppliers in other Member States and outside the EU only.
- Exempt acquisitions of goods not for resale, and reverse charges at 7%.
