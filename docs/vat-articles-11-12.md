# VAT registrations under Articles 11 and 12 in Malta

Legal basis of the record "Malta VAT Registration", of the tax categories of the registrations under Articles 11 and 12 of the Value Added Tax Act, of the reports "VAT Declaration Article 11 Malta" and "VAT Article 12 Malta" and of the rule register `malta_compliance/vat/rules.py`: who registers under each article, the turnover limits of a small enterprise with their periods of validity, the declarations, the payment dates of self-charged VAT, interest and penalties. Each statement cites the provision or the official guidance it rests on. Points that no source states are listed in [Readings](#readings) with the reading applied and its grounds. The return of a registration under Article 10 is described in [vat.md](vat.md).

## The registrations

- **Article 10**: a taxable person established in Malta registers within 30 days of the first supply other than an exempt without credit supply (art. 10(1)). It charges VAT, deducts input tax and files the return of [vat.md](vat.md).
- **Article 11**: a taxable person established in Malta that qualifies as a small enterprise "may apply to the Commissioner to be registered under this article" (art. 11(1)). Its supplies are exempt without credit (Fifth Schedule, Part Two, item 16(1)): no VAT is charged and no input tax is deducted. The registration takes effect on the first day of the month of the application or on the start of the activity, whichever is the later (art. 11(2)). The number has no prefix "MT" and "shall not be treated for any purposes of this Act as a value added tax identification number" (art. 13(3)).
- **Article 12**: a taxable person not registered under Article 10 registers for the operations listed under [Self-charged VAT](#self-charged-vat-under-article-12). The number has the prefix "MT" (art. 13(3)).
- **Article 11 with Article 12**: a person registered under Article 11 "shall also be required to register under article 12" when it makes taxable intra-community acquisitions, receives services whose VAT it is liable to pay, or supplies services in another Member State "for which VAT is payable solely by the recipient" (art. 11(10)). A registration under Article 12 ends with a registration under Article 10 (art. 12(10)(a)).
- **Documents of an Article 11 person**: a fiscal receipt for every supply taking place in Malta and no tax invoice (art. 51; Thirteenth Schedule, item 2; tax invoices FAQ: "an Article 11 registered person is never to issue a tax invoice"). A supply taking place outside Malta needs no fiscal receipt (Thirteenth Schedule, item 2B).

The record "Malta VAT Registration" of a company holds these registrations with the first and last day of each. Registrations under Articles 10 and 11 follow each other; one under Article 12 runs beside one under Article 11.

## Turnover limit of a small enterprise

### From 2025

- **Limit**: the "Domestic annual turnover" of the preceding calendar year is "not more than the Domestic threshold" at the application (Sixth Schedule, Part One, item 1), and a registered person continues to qualify "where his turnover during a calendar year does not exceed the Domestic threshold", losing the qualification "on the date the Domestic threshold is exceeded within a calendar year" (item 3). The Domestic threshold is 35,000 euro (definition 5).
- **Turnover**: "the total annual turnover from supplies of goods and services, exclusive of VAT, made by a taxable person taking place within Malta during a calendar year" (definition 4). It consists of the supplies that would be taxed if made by a person registered under Article 10, the exempt with credit transactions of items 1, 3, 5, 6, 7 and 8(1) of Part One of the Fifth Schedule, and immovable property, financial and insurance transactions unless ancillary (definition 3(1)). Disposals of capital assets are left out (definition 3(2)). "Only supplies which take place in Malta for VAT purposes" count (guidelines, p. 2).
- **Leaving**: the person applies for the cancellation within 15 days of the date on which it no longer qualifies (art. 11(5)(b)); the cancellation applies "from the first day of the calendar month following" that date (art. 11(7)), and the registration under Article 10 takes effect on the date of the cancellation (art. 10(6)(c) and (7a)). A person that does not apply is liable to a penalty of at most 500 euro (art. 40).

### Until 2024

- **Limit**: a person applying for a registration qualified with a turnover of the relevant period below the Entry Threshold of its activity, and a registered person as long as that turnover did not exceed the threshold of the table below (Sixth Schedule as in force until 2024, Part One, items 1 and 3). The Commissioner classified each activity as the supply of goods, the supply of services with a relatively low value added, or another activity (item 7 and Table); the record "Malta VAT Registration" holds the category in the field "Activity".
- **Relevant period**: "a period of twelve months ending on the last day of the calendar quarter preceding that in which that particular date falls"; for an activity started within these months, the quarters after the quarter of the start, with the threshold divided by four and multiplied by the number of these quarters (items 5 and 7).
- **Turnover**: "the total taxable value of all supplies" of the economic activity without exempt without credit supplies, transfers of a going concern, supplies treated as made to oneself and "the sale or disposal of fixed assets" (item 4(1)). Supplies taking place outside Malta counted (guidelines, example D).
- **No registration from 2011 to 2014**: a person established in Malta with a turnover of at most 7,000 euro in the calendar year was not registered under Article 11 and was released from issuing fiscal receipts (L.N. 524 of 2010, reg. 2, 3 and 5); the regulations were revoked from 2015, with a registration by 30 June 2015 (L.N. 67 of 2015).

## The declaration of Article 11

- **Form**: the "Statement of Sales/Purchases" with four boxes: 1 Sale of Goods, 2 Provision of Services, 3 Purchases of stock for re-sale, 4 Purchases of capital assets during the year (S.L. 406.09, Fifth Schedule; the online form of the MTCA portal holds the same boxes). It is submitted through the portal (Sixth Schedule, Part One, item 5(1)).
- **Period**: the calendar year; the first period starts with the registration, the last ends with its cancellation (item 5(2) to (4)).
- **Due date**: "by not later than the fifteenth (15th) day of the second (2) month following the month during which such period ends" (item 5(5)), which is 15 February. The portal shows 15 March for the period 2025, and the registration FAQ states that no penalty applies to a declaration submitted electronically by 22 March.
- **Penalty**: 10 euro for every month or part of a month, at most 120 euro for a declaration (art. 38(2)(a)).

## Self-charged VAT under Article 12

- **Acquisitions of goods**: a taxable person not registered under Article 10 registers when the value of its intra-community acquisitions in Malta in a calendar year exceeds the acquisitions threshold of 10,000 euro, by the date of the acquisition that exceeds it (art. 12(1); Third Schedule, Part Three, item 3). The value is taken without the VAT of the other Member State and without new means of transport and excise goods (item 4). The tax on acquisitions applies from the accession date, 1 May 2004 (art. 4(b)).
- **Services received**: a taxable person established in Malta registers "by not later than the date on which he receives a service" whose VAT it is liable to pay under art. 20(2) (art. 12(3), from 1 January 2010 by Act II of 2009). "There is no threshold applicable to the acquisition of services" (registration FAQ).
- **Services supplied**: a taxable person established in Malta registers by the date on which it supplies a service in another Member State whose VAT the recipient pays under Article 196 of Directive 2006/112/EC (art. 12(3a), from 1 January 2025).
- **Goods below the threshold**: a person holding a number for services stays outside the taxation of its acquisitions of goods; "if that taxable person communicates this VAT identification number to a supplier in respect of an intra-Community acquisition of goods, he shall be deemed to have exercised the option" to tax them (Regulation (EU) No 282/2011, art. 4). A registration by option holds for two calendar years (art. 12(7)).
- **Tax**: the payment of the tax on these supplies is "a liability of the person to whom the supply is made" (art. 20(2)), at the Maltese rate of the goods or services (art. 19). An acquisition of Part Three of the Fifth Schedule, such as food, is exempt. "There is no mechanism for the recovery of VAT by article 12 registered persons" (registration FAQ).
- **Notice of payment**: each purchase is reported with the date of its invoice, the date of the acquisition or supply, its value and its VAT, services marked as received from the EU or from outside it (S.L. 406.09, Sixth Schedule, VAT Form 004/2010; online since 2020).
- **Payment dates**: the VAT on an acquisition "by not later than the fifteenth day of the month following that during which it becomes chargeable" (art. 21(2)), where it becomes chargeable on the date of the invoice or, where earlier, on "the fifteenth day of the month following the date of the acquisition" (Fourth Schedule, item 6). The VAT on a service "by not later than the fifteenth day of the second month next following either the date of invoice or of the month during which the consignment or the supply of services is received, as the case may be, whichever is the earlier" (art. 21(3)).
- **Annual declaration**: the total value and the total VAT of the services received from the EU, of the services received from outside the EU and of the intra-community acquisitions of goods (S.L. 406.09, Seventh Schedule, VAT Form 005/2010; art. 30A).
- **Interest and penalties**: interest for each month or part of a month in which the tax remains unpaid (art. 21(4)) at the rate of S.L. 406.19; 20% of an understated tax (art. 37A); 10 euro for every month of a late declaration, at most 120 euro (art. 38(2)(a)); the penalty of art. 39(2) for a late application for the registration.
- **Recapitulative statement**: required of persons registered under Article 10 (art. 30(2)). For a person registered under Article 11 with a number under Article 12, "the submission of a recapitulative statement shall not be required" (services notes, scenario 2). Until 2024 every taxable person submitted it (art. 30(3) as then in force).

A category of Article 12 names the origin, the kind and the rate: "VAT Art 12 EU services", "VAT Art 12 Non-EU services" at 18%, 5% and 12%, "VAT Art 12 EU goods" and "VAT Art 12 EU goods for re-sale" at 18% and 5%, and "VAT Art 12 EU capital goods 18%". Its template books the VAT on 5960 Non-recoverable VAT and deducts the same amount on 2220 Output VAT Reverse Charge, so that the invoice total stays the amount payable to the supplier; for a fixed asset the first row is booked on the fixed asset account of the asset, as for the return of Article 10 ([vat.md](vat.md), "Non-deductible VAT in the books"). A payment to the Commissioner is a debit on 2220.

The report "VAT Article 12 Malta" lists the purchases of a calendar year as in the notice of payment, with the due date of each, applies the debits on 2220 from vouchers other than Purchase Invoices to them in the order of the due dates, and totals the three lines of the annual declaration.

## Readings

Points that no source states, with the reading applied by the app and its grounds.

| Point | Reading | Grounds |
|---|---|---|
| Due date of the Article 11 declaration | 15 March of the following year | The MTCA portal shows this date for the period 2025; the Sixth Schedule names 15 February, and the registration FAQ accepts an electronic submission until 22 March without penalty |
| Due date of the annual declaration of Article 12 | 15 March of the following year | Art. 30A leaves the date to the form; the date of the Article 11 declaration is applied |
| Supplies in boxes 1 and 2 | Every sale of the economic activity, with the supplies taking place outside Malta | The declaration relates "to his economic activity" (art. 30(1)) and is a "Statement of Sales/Purchases"; the limit of 35,000 euro alone is restricted to supplies in Malta |
| Amounts in boxes 3 and 4 | The amount paid, with the VAT in it; a purchase under Article 12 at its net total plus the self-charged VAT | A person registered under Article 11 deducts no VAT, so the VAT is part of the cost of the purchase |
| Sale of a capital asset | In box 1, outside the turnover of the limit | Box 1 holds the sale of goods; definition 3(2) of the Sixth Schedule leaves disposals of capital assets out of the turnover |
| Supplies between the date the limit is exceeded and the registration under Article 10 | Without VAT | The cancellation applies from the first day of the following month (art. 11(7)) and the registration under Article 10 from the date of the cancellation (art. 10(7a)); item 16(1) of the Fifth Schedule, Part Two, exempts the supplies of a person "entitled to be so registered", and no guidance states the treatment of these days |
| Exempt purchases in the notice and the declaration of Article 12 | Left out | The forms hold services "on which VAT is due in Malta" and acquisitions "on which VAT is paid in Malta" |
| Start of the limits of Act X of 2003 | 2004-05-01 | The Act came into force by a notice in the Gazette. Act II of 2004 amends the article 19 of the original Act with effect from 2004-01-01, and L.N. 158 of 2004 prescribes the forms of the new articles from 2004-05-01, the accession date |
| Limit of other activities from 2021-01-01 to 2021-06-30 | 14,000 euro | L.N. 163 of 2018 applied "unless renewed" until 2020-12-31 (reg. 1(3)); L.N. 463 of 2020 set 30,000 euro from 2021-07-01 |
| Limits in Maltese liri | Converted at 0.4293 liri per euro | The irrevocable conversion rate of Council Regulation (EC) No 1134/2007; the books of a company of the app are in euro |
| Due date of the VAT on a service | The 15th day of the second month after the month of the invoice or, where earlier, of the supply | Art. 21(3) names "the second month next following either the date of invoice or of the month" of the supply; VAT Form 004/2010 repeats it |
| Dates of a purchase under Article 12 | The posting date is the date of the acquisition or supply, the supplier invoice date the date of the invoice | The fields of a Purchase Invoice; the annual declaration takes the purchases acquired or received in the year |
| Value of an acquisition outside Article 12 | The amount as booked, with the VAT of the other Member State | The invoice is booked gross; the threshold counts the value without that VAT, so the report reaches it earlier than the law |

## Not covered

- The registrations under Article 11A and Article 11B: the exemption in other Member States, its quarterly declaration and the Union threshold of 100,000 euro.
- Exempt without credit supplies of a person registered under Article 11, and immovable property, financial and insurance transactions in the turnover of the limit.
- The turnover of related persons of a body of persons (Sixth Schedule, Part One, item 4), and the conditions of a change from Article 10 to Article 11.
- New means of transport and excise goods (art. 15), non-taxable legal persons, and supplies of goods in Malta whose VAT the customer pays under art. 20(2)(a) or (c).
- A capital asset bought as a service from abroad in box 4, and self-charged VAT at 7%.
- Interest and penalties: the reports name the rates and compute no amounts.
- The penalty for a late declaration before 2008.
